## Notes
- **CC BY-SA 2.0 (Eberhard photo):** the adapted photo (crop + desaturation) carries the same CC BY-SA 2.0 license. Putting it in a video alongside other material doesn't relicense the video as a whole.
- **Mixkit:** the Free licenses allow commercial use, including monetized YouTube, with no attribution required. Six candidate clips carried the *Restricted* (non-commercial) license, so they were rejected and replaced (see `13_PROJECT_FILES/PRODUCTION_NOTES.md`).
- **Library of Congress:** the Bain Collection item 2014684845 is marked "No known restrictions on publication" and was published c. 1890.
- **Not used:** a CC BY photo captioned "with Tesla co-founder Marc Tarpenning" shows two people and doesn't say which one is Tarpenning. Guessing would risk mislabelling a real person, so his card uses a monogram instead.
