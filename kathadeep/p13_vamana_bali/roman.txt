Zara sochiye,/ ek chhota sa braahman baalak.../ aur usne do kadmon mein,/ poora brahmaand naap liya./ Aaj Bali Pratipada hai./ Aur ye kahani hai Raja Bali ki./ Bhagavat Puraan kehta hai,/ Prahlad ke pote,/ Asurraj Bali ne,/ teenon lok jeet liye the./ Devta swarg se beghar ho gaye./ Tab Mata Aditi ke ghar,/ Bhagwan Vishnu ne janm liya,/ Vaaman roop mein./ Chhota sa kad,/ haath mein chhatra aur kamandal./ Bali us samay Narmada ke kinaare,/ ek bada yagya kar rahe the./ Unhone Vaaman se kaha,/ jo chaho, maang lo./ Vaaman bole,/ Rajan, mujhe bas teen kadam zameen chahiye./ Bali hanse,/ bas itna?/ Tabhi Guru Shukracharya ne roka,/ Bali, ye saadhaaran baalak nahi,/ swayam Vishnu hain!/ Ye sab chheen lenge./ Par Bali bole,/ jo vachan de diya,/ woh main nahi todunga./ Aur jaise hi sankalp ka jal gira,/ Vaaman badhne lage.../ badhte gaye.../ aakaash tak./ Pehle kadam mein poori prithvi./ Doosre kadam mein poora swarg./ Aur teesra kadam?/ Rakhne ki jagah hi nahi bachi./ Tab Bali ne sir jhukaaya,/ Prabhu, teesra kadam mere sir par rakhiye./ Bhagwan prasann hue./ Bali ko Sutal Lok ka raaj diya,/ aur kaha,/ main hamesha tumhare dwaar par khada rahunga./ Lok-maanyata hai,/ aaj ke din,/ Bali apni praja se milne aate hain./ Aaj raat,/ jab Brahma ji ne Krishna ki pareeksha li./ Follow kar lijiye.
