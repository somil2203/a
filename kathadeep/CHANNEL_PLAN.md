## 3. How to start earning (in order of what's realistic soonest)

**Step 0 — set up now (free, 15 minutes)**
- Instagram: switch to a **Creator/Professional account** (it is, since vidIQ shows owner insights) → Professional Dashboard → **Monetisation status / Eligibility**. That screen tells you which programmes your account can use: Gifts, Subscriptions, Bonuses, Branded content. Availability in India changes, so trust the screen over any blog.
- Put a **link in bio** (Linktree or similar) with: YouTube channel, WhatsApp/Telegram channel, and later your products.
- **Cross-post every Reel to YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels** (same file). YouTube is where real ad revenue is. YouTube Partner Programme thresholds: **1,000 subscribers + 10 million Shorts views in 90 days** (or 4,000 watch hours) for ad-revenue sharing; the lower tier (500 subs + 3M Shorts views) unlocks fan funding (Super Thanks, memberships).

**Step 1 — affiliate (can start from ~2–5k followers)**
- Amazon Associates / Flipkart affiliate links for things the stories naturally point to: Gita Press books (Kathopanishad, Valmiki Ramayan, Bhagavat), clay diyas, puja thalis, kids' mythology books. Mention them in the pinned comment ("Kathopanishad Gita Press edition: link in bio").

**Step 2 — brand collaborations (from ~10k engaged followers)**
- Natural fits: devotional/astrology apps, puja-samagri brands, spiritual book publishers, ayurveda brands (Dhanvantari episode!), kids' learning apps.
- Prepare a 1-page media kit: followers, average Reel views, audience age/city split (from Professional Dashboard → Insights), 3 best Reels. Rate starting point many Indian creators use: roughly ₹0.5–1 per average Reel view for an integrated story mention; negotiate from your real numbers.
- Mark paid posts with Instagram's **Paid partnership** label.

**Step 3 — your own products (strongest long-term)**
- **E-book / PDF**: "Deepotsav ki 5 Kahaniyan" or "Upanishad ki 10 Kahaniyan" (bilingual, with the scripture references we already keep). Sell via Instamojo / Gumroad / Topmate.
- **Kids' bedtime audio stories** (same Rudra voice + score): sell as a pack or a monthly subscription. Parents are a strong buying audience for Sanatan stories.
- **Instagram Subscriptions** (if eligible): subscriber-only extended versions ("Nachiketa: poora samvad").
