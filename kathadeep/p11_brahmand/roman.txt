Maa ne bete se kaha,/ munh kholo./ Aur us chhote se munh mein.../ poora brahmaand tha./ Gokul ki baat hai./ Balram aur gwaal-baal daudte hue/ Yashoda Maiya ke paas aaye,/ Maiya, Kanha ne mitti khaayi hai!/ Yashoda ne Kanha ka haath pakda,/ aur daanta,/ kyun khaayi mitti?/ Kanha bole,/ Maa, maine nahi khaayi./ Ye sab jhooth bol rahe hain./ Vishwaas na ho,/ toh mera munh dekh lo./ Aur Kanha ne munh khola./ Bhagavat Puraan kehta hai,/ Yashoda ne us munh mein dekha.../ aakaash, dishaayein,/ parvat, dweep, samudra./ Poori prithvi./ Agni, vaayu,/ chandrama, taare./ Aur usi mein... Gokul./ Aur usi Gokul mein,/ khud Yashoda,/ Kanha ke saamne khadi./ Yashoda kaanp gayin./ Ye sapna hai?/ Maaya hai?/ Ya mera beta.../ swayam Bhagwan hai?/ Tabhi Kanha ne apni maaya phailaayi./ Yashoda sab bhool gayin,/ aur bete ko phir se god mein utha liya./ Sochiye,/ jiske munh mein poora brahmaand hai,/ woh Maa ki god mein,/ bas ek bachcha bankar rehna chahta tha./ Agli kahani,/ Yamuna ka zehreela naag,/ Kaliya./ Follow kar lijiye.
