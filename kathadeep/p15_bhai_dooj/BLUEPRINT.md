# Bhai Dooj · Din 5 — reference forensics → creative blueprint → our execution

Reference: `Video-62232.mp4` (viral, same niche, same topic). 102.6 s · 720×1280 · 30 fps · −14.1 LUFS · Hindi male VO.
Ours: `p15_bhai_dooj` · 110.3 s · 1080×1920 · 30 fps · −14 LUFS target · ElevenLabs Rudra (series voice) · original script.

## 1. Reference breakdown
**Hook (0–7 s).** Frame 1: Yama on his buffalo before hell's gate, which opens. Text builds word by word: a shock claim, then
a 3-second twist that turns terror into something homely (he goes to his sister's for dinner). Contrast + curiosity in < 4 s.

**Story architecture.** hook → context card (calendar, Day 5) → source credibility (two Puranas as manuscript props) →
characters (children of Surya; Yama → judge of the dead; Yamuna → river) → conflict loop (daily invitations; "today? tomorrow?
day after…") → turning point (Kartik Shukla Dvitiya, she insists; hook image returns) → ritual, item by item with labels →
boon → viewer question with a strikethrough list → her selfless two-point wish on a note → "Tathastu" → insight reframe →
practical utility (date/muhurat) → full-circle series callback → share CTA ("send to your brother/sister").

**Typography.** No bottom subtitles. Only 1–2 lines of condensed heavy caps, top-left, built word by word; line 1 white/black,
line 2 red/gold accent; ~3 words a line, 2 lines max. Small mono labels in black pills (sources, places, "Day 5"). Paper
props: torn notes, calendar, cards, red strikethroughs, stamps. Fixed series tag top-right.

**Visuals.** AI-painted illustrations with consistent characters (green-skinned Yama, Yamuna in a blue saree), placed inside a
paper-collage motion-graphics layer (calendar, manuscripts, map with a dotted path). No hard cuts (scene detection finds none):
continuous camera pushes, prop pop-ins, text builds — a new visual event every 2–3 s.

**Audio.** Calm, intimate VO; gentle bed; light SFX on prop pops and text builds; impacts only at the hook and the boon.

## 2. The viral formula (10 levers) — and how we reproduce each
1. Shock-contrast first line with a matching frame-1 image → "Maut ke devta… khud kisi ke ghar khaana khaane" over Yama at Yamaloka's gate.
2. Twist within 3 s → whip from the gate to Yama arriving at a small lit home ("KHAANA KHAANE!").
3. Source on screen early → Padma Purana manuscript prop + mono pill at 18 s.
4. Conflict loop that builds → invitation cards ("घर आओ भैया — Yamuna") land and pile up; NYOTA counter 1→99; "AAJ NAHI. KAL… PHIR KABHI."
5. Callback → the dotted road from Yamaloka's gate to her door at the turning point.
6. A visual event every 2–3 s → props, pills, caption builds, camera pushes, one close-up (the tilak).
7. Viewer question + strikethrough → "AAP HOTE TOH KYA MAANGTE?" SONA? / SWARG? / AMARTA? struck out.
8. Insight reframe → our own: Dhanteras (Nachiketa at Yama's door) vs today (Yama at his sister's door).
9. Utility → calendar 11 Nov ticked/circled (no muhurat claims: sources disagree by city).
10. Share trigger → "SAVE KARO · POORI SERIES" + "BHAI / BEHEN KO BHEJO" strips; all five diyas lit ("POORI!").

## 3. Factual guardrails
Only claims that the sources agree on: Yama & Yamuna are Surya's children; Yama = Dharmaraja (judge of deeds); Yamuna the river;
Yamuna's repeated invitations; on Kartika Shukla Dvitiya he came; aarti, tilak, food; the boon — a brother who eats at his
sister's home on this day need not fear Yama. Not used: "Yama emptied hell", chapter-and-verse quotes, muhurat times.

## 5. Known limitation (stated honestly)
The reference's characters are AI-painted. No image-generation API is connected to this studio, so our characters are our own
vector illustrations (code-drawn). The editorial/collage layer, typography, pacing, story architecture and sound grammar match
the reference closely; the painted texture does not.
