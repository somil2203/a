Maut ke devta,/ jinke darwaaze se koi lautkar nahi aata.../ saal mein ek din,/ khud kisi ke darwaaze par,/ khaana khaane jaate hain./ Aaj Bhai Dooj hai,/ Deepotsav ka paanchva aur aakhiri diya./ Aur ye kahani hai,/ usi darwaaze ki./ Padma Puraan mein ye katha aati hai./ Surya Dev ki do santaanein thin,/ Yam aur Yamuna./ Bhai aur behen./ Yam bane Dharmaraj,/ har jeev ke karmon ka hisaab,/ unhi ke haath mein./ Aur Yamuna,/ dharti par nadi bankar behne lagin./ Yamuna baar-baar bhai ko bulaatin,/ Bhaiya, kabhi mere ghar aao,/ saath mein bhojan karo./ Par Yam ke paas samay kahaan tha?/ Har din,/ anginat jeevon ka hisaab./ Aaj nahi.../ kal.../ phir kabhi./ Nyote par nyote aate rahe./ Phir aayi Kartik Shukla Dwitiya./ Aur is baar,/ Yamraj mana nahi kar paaye./ Yamuna ne aarti utaari,/ maathe par tilak lagaaya,/ aur apne haathon se bhojan parosa./ Yamraj itne prasann hue,/ ki bole,/ behen, jo chaho maang lo./ Aap hote,/ toh kya maangte?/ Sona?/ Swarg?/ Amarta?/ Yamuna ne apne liye,/ kuch nahi maanga./ Bolin,/ har saal isi din,/ aap mere ghar aana./ Aur jo bhai aaj apni behen ke ghar bhojan kare,/ use aapka bhay na ho./ Yamraj bole,/ tathaastu./ Sochiye,/ Dhanteras par,/ Nachiketa Yamraj ke dwaar gaya tha./ Aur aaj,/ khud Yamraj,/ behen ke dwaar par khade hain./ Paanch din,/ paanch diye,/ paanch kahaniyaan,/ aaj poori huin./ Ye reel apne bhai ya behen ko bhejiye.
