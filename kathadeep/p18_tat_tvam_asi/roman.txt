Baarah saal padhkar beta ghar lauta.../ aur pita ke ek sawaal par,/ chup ho gaya./ Chhandogya Upanishad./ Rishi Uddalak ka beta Shvetaketu,/ saare Ved padhkar lauta,/ aur ghamand se bhara./ Pita ne poochha,/ kya tumne woh seekha,/ jisse ansuna sunaai de,/ aur anjaana jaana jaaye?/ Shvetaketu bola,/ nahi./ Aap hi sikhaaiye./ Pita bole,/ is paani mein namak daalo,/ aur subah laana./ Subah bole,/ ab namak nikaalo./ Namak dikha hi nahi./ Upar se chakho,/ khaara./ Beech se,/ khaara./ Neeche se,/ khaara./ Pita bole,/ dikhta nahi,/ par har boond mein hai./ Waise hi ek sookshm satya,/ is poore jagat mein hai./ Wahi satya hai,/ wahi aatma hai,/ Tat Tvam Asi,/ Shvetaketu./ Woh tum ho./ Upanishad ki agli kahani,/ jab ek stree ne,/ Raja ki sabha mein,/ sabse bade Rishi se sawaal kiye.
