Yamuna ka paani aisa zehreela tha,/ ki upar udte pakshi bhi gir jaate the./ Aur ek baalak.../ usi paani mein kood gaya./ Bhagavat Puraan kehta hai,/ Yamuna ke ek kund mein rehta tha Kaliya naag./ Kai phanon wala.../ zehar ugalta hua./ Ek din gwaal-baal aur gaayon ne woh paani pee liya,/ aur behosh hokar gir pade./ Krishna ne apni amrit drishti se,/ sabko phir se jeevit kar diya./ Phir Krishna Kadamb ke ped par chadhe.../ aur zehreele paani mein chhalaang laga di./ Kaliya ne unhe apni kundali mein jakad liya./ Kinaare par poora Braj ro raha tha./ Nand Baba, Yashoda Maiya.../ sirf Balram shaant the./ Woh jaante the,/ unka bhai kaun hai./ Tabhi Krishna ne apna shareer phailaaya./ Kundali dheeli padi,/ aur Krishna uchhalkar,/ Kaliya ke phanon par ja chadhe./ Aur phir,/ nritya!/ Jo phan uthta,/ usi par Krishna ka charan padta./ Kaliya thakkar choor ho gaya./ Tab naag-patniyaan haath jodkar aayin,/ Prabhu, ise kshama kar dijiye./ Krishna ne Kaliya ko chhod diya,/ aur kaha,/ Yamuna chhodkar,/ samudra ke Ramanak dweep chale jaao./ Aur ek vardaan bhi,/ tumhare phanon par mere charanon ke nishaan hain./ Ab Garud bhi tumhe nahi chhuega./ Kal subah,/ chhappan bhog hi kyun?/ Follow kar lijiye.
