Guru ne poochha,/ tumhare pita kaun hain?/ Aur us bachche ne,/ woh sach bol diya.../ jo koi bhi chhipa leta./ Chhandogya Upanishad ki kahani hai./ Satyakaam naam ka ek baalak,/ Ved padhna chahta tha./ Usne Maa Jabala se poochha,/ Maa, mera gotra kya hai?/ Maa bolin,/ Beta, jawaani mein main daasi thi,/ kai gharon mein kaam kiya./ Mujhe nahi pata,/ tera gotra kya hai./ Main Jabala hoon,/ tu Satyakaam hai,/ kehna, Satyakaam Jaabaal./ Satyakaam Guru Gautam ke paas gaye./ Guru ne wahi poochha./ Aur Satyakaam ne,/ bina kuch chhipaaye,/ Maa ki baat jyon ki tyon keh di./ Guru bole,/ itna sach,/ keval Braahman hi bol sakta hai./ Jaao, samidha laao,/ main tumhe deeksha doonga./ Phir Guru ne use chaar sau dubli,/ kamzor gaayein deen./ Satyakaam bole,/ jab tak ye hazaar nahi hotin,/ main nahi lautunga./ Aur jab gaayein hazaar huin,/ toh ek bail,/ Agni,/ hans,/ aur ek jal-pakshi ne,/ use Brahm ka gyaan diya./ Janm se nahi,/ sach se./ Ye reel use bhejiye,/ jise aaj sach bolne ki himmat chahiye.
