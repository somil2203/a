Ek din Krishna ne.../ khud ko hi pranaam kiya./ Aur unka hi doosra roop,/ poore Braj ka bhog kha gaya./ Aaj Annakoot hai,/ Govardhan Pooja./ Aur aaj bante hain Chhappan Bhog./ Par chhappan hi kyun?/ Bhagavat Puraan mein Krishna ne Brajvaasiyon se kaha,/ Indra ke yagya ki saamagri se pakwaan banaiye./ Kheer, halwa, pue, pooriyaan.../ aur ye bhog Govardhan ko chadhaiye./ Tabhi hua adbhut./ Krishna ne ek vishaal roop dhaaran kiya,/ aur bole,/ main hi Govardhan hoon./ Aur woh saara bhog,/ us roop ne grahan kar liya./ Aur nanhe Krishna ne,/ Brajvaasiyon ke saath,/ usi roop ko pranaam kiya./ Yahi hai Annakoot,/ ann ka pahaad./ Ab chhappan ka hisaab./ Ye shaastra nahi,/ lok-maanyata hai./ Kehte hain,/ Kanha din mein aath baar bhojan karte the,/ aath pehar./ Par jab unhone saat din Govardhan uthaya,/ toh ek daana bhi nahi khaaya./ Baarish thami,/ toh Maa Yashoda aur Brajvaasiyon ne,/ saat din ke aathon pehar ka bhog,/ ek saath banaya./ Saat guna aath.../ chhappan./ Chhappan Bhog./ Toh aaj jab thaali sajaayein,/ yaad rakhiye,/ ye Bhagwan ki bhookh nahi,/ hamara prem hai./ Kal,/ paanchva aur aakhiri diya,/ Bhai Dooj./ Yamraj apni behen ke ghar kyun gaye?/ Kal.
