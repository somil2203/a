Aapka har karm.../ is waqt bhi,/ kahin likha ja raha hai./ Sawaal hai,/ likhta kaun hai?/ Aaj subah humne dekha,/ Yamraj behen ke ghar gaye./ Par Yamlok mein,/ unke saath baithte hain,/ unke lekhaakaar./ Chitragupt./ Katha kehti hai,/ Yamraj ne Brahma ji se kaha,/ anginat jeev hain./ Sabke karmon ka hisaab,/ main akele kaise rakhoon?/ Tab Brahma ji dhyaan mein baith gaye./ Aur bahut samay baad,/ unki kaaya se ek divya purush prakat hua,/ haath mein kalam,/ aur dawaat./ Brahma bole,/ tum meri kaaya se janme ho,/ isliye Kaayasth kahlaaoge./ Aur mere bheetar gupt the,/ isliye naam hoga,/ Chitragupt./ Tab se Chitragupt,/ har jeev ka har karm likhte hain./ Aur usi lekhe par,/ Yamraj faisla sunaate hain./ Isiliye aaj,/ Yam Dwitiya par,/ Chitragupt Pooja hoti hai./ Aur parampara hai,/ kalam aur dawaat ki pooja./ Toh agli baar,/ kuch bhi karne se pehle sochiyega.../ kalam chal rahi hai.
