Ek poore saal tak,/ Braj ki har maa ke ghar,/ uska beta nahi.../ khud Krishna laut-te rahe./ Aur kisi ko pata tak nahi chala./ Bhagavat Puraan kehta hai,/ ek din Yamuna kinaare,/ Krishna gwaal-baalon ke saath bhojan kar rahe the./ Bachhde charte-charte door nikal gaye./ Krishna bachhdon ko dhoondhne gaye./ Ye sab dekh rahe the,/ srishti ke rachayita,/ Brahma ji./ Unhone socha,/ dekhoon, ye baalak sach mein Bhagwan hai?/ Brahma ne saare bachhde,/ aur saare gwaal-baal,/ ek gufa mein chhipa diye./ Krishna laute,/ na bachhde, na dost./ Woh samajh gaye,/ ye kiska khel hai./ Aur tab Krishna ne woh kiya,/ jo koi soch bhi nahi sakta./ Woh khud.../ har bachhda ban gaye./ Har gwaal-baal ban gaye./ Wahi chehre,/ wahi laathiyaan,/ wahi baansuri,/ wahi shararatein./ Shaam ko har bachhda apni gaay ke paas gaya,/ har baalak apni maa ke paas./ Aur maaon ka pyaar.../ pehle se bhi zyada badh gaya./ Aise poora ek saal beet gaya./ Phir Brahma laute,/ aur dekha,/ gufa mein bhi sab hain.../ aur Krishna ke saath bhi sab hain!/ Aur phir unhone dekha,/ har baalak, har bachhda.../ chaturbhuj Vishnu ban gaya./ Brahma ka ghamand choor hua./ Woh Krishna ke charanon mein gir pade./ Aur jab dost gufa se laute,/ toh bole,/ Kanha, itni jaldi aa gaye?/ Humne toh abhi ek kaur bhi nahi khaaya!/ Unke liye ek saal.../ bas ek pal tha./ Kal,/ Deepotsav ka aakhiri diya,/ Bhai Dooj./ Follow kar lijiye.
