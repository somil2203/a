Raja ki sabha mein,/ hazaar gaayein khadi thin,/ har seeng par sona./ Aur ek stree khadi hui,/ sabse bade Rishi se sawaal karne./ Brihadaranyak Upanishad./ Raja Janak bole,/ jo sabse bada brahmagyaani ho,/ ye gaayein le jaaye./ Rishi Yagyavalkya ne shishya se kaha,/ gaayein haank le chalo./ Sabha bhadak uthi,/ aur prashn shuru hue./ Phir uthin,/ Gaargi./ Poochha,/ sab kuch jal mein buna hai,/ toh jal kismein?/ Vaayu mein./ Vaayu kismein?/ Aakaash.../ aur aage, aur aage./ Yagyavalkya bole,/ Gaargi, itna mat poochho,/ kahin tumhara sir na gir jaaye./ Gaargi baith gayin.../ par haari nahi./ Phir uthin,/ jaise yoddha do baan lekar aata hai,/ main do prashn laayi hoon./ Jo aakaash ke upar,/ dharti ke neeche,/ bhoot, vartmaan, bhavishya,/ woh kismein buna hai?/ Uttar mila,/ Akshar mein./ Us avinaashi mein./ Aur Gaargi ne hi sabha se kaha,/ Brahm ki charcha mein,/ inhe koi nahi hara sakta.
