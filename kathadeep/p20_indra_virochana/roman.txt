Ek sawaal ka jawaab paane mein,/ Indra ko ek sau ek saal lage./ Chhandogya Upanishad./ Prajapati ne kaha,/ jo aatma ko jaan le,/ use sab mil jaata hai./ Devon ne Indra ko bheja,/ asuron ne Virochan ko./ Battees saal,/ dono ne seva ki./ Prajapati bole,/ paani ke paatra mein dekho./ Jo dikhe,/ wahi aatma hai./ Dono ne saj-dhajkar dekha,/ apna hi shareer dikha./ Virochan santusht hokar laut gaya,/ shareer hi aatma hai./ Aur asuron ko yahi sikhaaya./ Par Indra raaste mein ruke,/ shareer toh boodha hota hai,/ marta hai./ Ye aatma kaise?/ Woh laute./ Phir battees saal./ Phir battees./ Phir paanch./ Kul ek sau ek saal,/ tab jaana,/ shareer nahi,/ jo ismein dekhta hai,/ wahi aatma hai./ Jawaab jaldi mil jaaye,/ toh rukkar sochiye,/ kahin woh Virochan wala jawaab toh nahi?
