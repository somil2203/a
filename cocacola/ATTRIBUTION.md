## Notes
- **Trademarks:** the only Coca-Cola script that appears is inside the genuine 1893 registration document and the 1890s ad, both used as historical evidence. The video's own titles use neutral typography and never imitate the logo.
- **Coca leaves:** the generic leaf B-roll on Mixkit wasn't coca, so the leaves on screen are either real *Erythroxylum coca* photos or a clearly stylised 3D render.
